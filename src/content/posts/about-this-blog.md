This blog is a place to store and expose my ideas and projects.

You will find explanations of concepts I ponder and tutorials and guides on tools and systems I use.

I write this blog to force myself to structure thoughts in an simple and consistent way, covering all the knowledge gaps that go unnoticed when I keep them in my head. This way I expect them to come out more fluent and easier to understand whenever I refer to them in the future.

I could write all these and keep it in private, but I decided to expose it to the world to connect with likeminded people. Also, it’s a way to communicate concisely with people I already know, so that they can see what I do, how I do it and, hopefully, why I do it.

Hope you enjoy!
